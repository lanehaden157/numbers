## Corpus

morphhb 2.0.2 (`package.json` + lock). `python -m biblecore corpus` reports
1,289 verses, 16,422 words, 159 paragraph breaks (94 petuḥah, 65 setumah).
English editions count 1,288 verses; English numbering differs from the Hebrew
in chapters 16, 17, 25, 29, 30. Both are known OSHB traits, explained in
`numbers-versification-map.md`. Count drifts on re-fetch → flag loudly.

Generated by `corpus`, don't edit: `Numbers-reading.txt`, `Numbers-words.tsv`,
`candidate-boundaries.md`, `numbers-versification.md`. They keep Hebrew
numbering; core converts to English when it reads them.
