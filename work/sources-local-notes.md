# Numbers v2: local sources (indexed 2026-11-25)

Converters in `tools/sources/` (Leviticus v2's constable, jps, tyndale, morales copied and adapted; levine, doty, whitt, rashi_local new). Copies of the Downloads files sit in `sources/downloads/<key>/` (originals untouched, Python run with `-I`). Rebuild one: `python -I tools/sources/<name>.py [args]`, then `python tools/split_sources.py <key>`. Whitt reads `sources/marked/jps.txt` and `candidate-boundaries.md`: build jps first. Native Hebrew: only in `rashi` (lemmas) and a few OCR-garbled lines in Levine; keep out of chat and reader files. `sefaria.py`'s default key list includes `rashi`: pass explicit keys so it does not overwrite the local copy.

- `levine-1` Levine, Numbers 1-20 (AB 4A, 1993): `abNumbers1-20 LevineBA 1993-opt.txt`, 1.3 MB OCR. Hebrew numbering. Indexed: intro (5 lettered parts), per unit the opening essay, translation by verse, notes by verse, COMMENT essays (range = the unit). Pages: `[p. 45-494]`, 409 markers (pp. 1-44 of the intro unmarked). 590 of 736 verses have their own translation section (the rest sit inside the previous verse's text); 428 notes, all 20 ch. OCR: digits/O/I swaps, a few Hebrew lines turned to symbols; verse splitting follows number order, so a lost verse number glues two verses. Front-matter copy of the whole translation and maps/indexes not indexed (the body repeats it).
- `levine-2` Levine, Numbers 21-36 (AB 4B, 2000): `Numbers 21-36 (Baruch A. Levine) (z-library...).txt`, 1.6 MB OCR. Same layout, all 16 ch. Pages `[p. 35-578]`, 478 markers (some gaps). 380 of 552 verses own translation section; 316 notes. Weaker than vol 1: glued verse numbers ("2~ereupon"), more splits missed (ch 22, 33, 35 worst). Balaam: 22:2-24:25 comments are one 68 KB essay.
- `constable` Constable, Notes on Numbers: the Downloads PDF read with pymupdf (the .txt in Downloads is a 2-up scan with page-number noise). It is the 2015 edition, not 2026. 123 sections, all ch, `[p. 2-123]`. Verse lines are his own: stretches with no verse line (e.g. 23:27-24:14) were folded into the section above and widened to the next heading. Scripture-reference lines ("Lev. 1:1") are no longer taken as headings.
- `whitt` Whitt, Numbers, new translation with commentary (2022): `Bible/William Whitt.txt`, archive.org OCR of a PDF. English chapter numbers only, because he prints no verse numbers. Translation: 36 chapter sections placed by length proportion against the JPS text with 14 name landmarks (naziyr 6:2, Qorah 16:1, Bil'am 22:5 ...), padded 20 lines each side: edges can be off by up to half a chapter, so verse-level reading needs the neighbouring chapter too. 553 notes, keyed by his paragraph label (P15,4c) and mapped to verses through the morphhb petuhah list; Aleppo vs Leningrad breaks drift the numbering by 1-2 paragraphs (P47 and P88 checked), so notes are indexed by whole chapters, the approximate verse range is in the title. Masoretic marks kept as he prints them: petuhah = a line `**` (the OCR turned his asterisks into noise like "Ey", "+"; every such lone short line between blank lines was rewritten, so treat `**` as "break near here", ~93 in the book); setumah = lines starting with an em-dash (kept as printed). Margin note letters (a, b, c ...) stay in the text; P-numbers and [Ch.] labels were stripped. Printed pages `[p. 91-200]` for notes and the composition-history essay only (translation pages carry no page numbers in the OCR).
- `rashi` Rashi on Numbers (Rosenbaum-Silbermann, Sefaria export): `Rashi on Numbers - en - merged.txt`. 687 verse sections, all 36 ch, Hebrew numbering, ~19k Hebrew letters (lemmas). Clean. Free, public domain.
- `doty` Doty, Numbers (Bible Study Textbook Series, College Press, 1973): identified as `Downloads/Numbers.txt` (+ `Numbers.pdf`, 422 pp scan). Units of TEXT (KJV), PARAPHRASE, COMMENTARY, QUESTIONS; 101 sections indexed by the verses of the TEXT block (OCR slips tolerated, gaps filled to the next unit). English numbering, all ch. `[p. N]` on about every second page (16-384); the scan prints the number at the foot, so the marker opens the next page. Weak spots: "w." for "vv." in headings, spaced digits in "Chapter 1 O : l", some units start with no title.
- `morales-exodus` Morales, Exodus Old and New (ESBT 2020): reused Leviticus v2's extracted text of the same PDF (`[pdf p. N]`, 300 markers). Biblical theology, not commentary: 41 sections are `intro`; only the daily-service section is indexed (28:1-8). Numbers cited inside (use `kit.py grep`): 6:22-27 and 3:7-8 (ch. 7), 11:16-17, 25, 29 and 12:6-8 (ch. 6, 10-11), 9:1-14 (ch. 5), 14:17-19, 16:3, 20:3, 11 (ch. 10-11). The Scripture index (printed pages) is in the file.
- `jps` JPS gender-sensitive Tanakh (Sefaria, `Numbers - en - merged.plain.txt`): 1288 verses, Hebrew numbering. Export quirks fixed: "G OD" on its own line, continuation lines, and two note-only lines (17:1, 17:16). 200 notes pulled out as [note ...].
- `tyndale` Tyndale TOTHT tagged Hebrew: 1288 verses, 16,423 words, English numbering; transliteration is rough (gloss only).

Found, not indexed: `Review_of_Jacob_Milgrom_Numbers_JPS_Tora.pdf` (a review, not a source); `Personal/Biblical Texts/Torah/Numbers - he - merged.plain.txt` (Sefaria Hebrew text; Tyndale covers it); `source-artifacts/numbers_0[1-4]_translation.html` (our own studies); Downloads `numbers_01/02_*.md` (earlier project notes); `archive/` is empty; the Torah folder's Cassuto/Sarna/Philo files are not Numbers; `Deuteronomy.pdf` and the JPS Deuteronomy .txt belong to that book.
