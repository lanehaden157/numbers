## Calls for Lane (1)

1. 1:2, nasaʾ roʾsh. Not ruled. Options: **lift the head** (recommended: literal, keeps the link to Exod 30:12 audible; Levine and Ibn Ezra read it as the census idiom); take a count (smoother English, hides the link).
2. 1:16, ʾalpe yisraʾel. Not ruled. Options: **heads of the thousands of Yisrael** (recommended: matches the census numerals; Ibn Ezra, Ellicott, Constable); heads of the clans of Yisrael (Levine's "sibs"; the word's older social sense).
3. 1:18, wa-yityaldu (a form found only here). Not ruled. Options: **registered their descent** (recommended: neutral between Rashi's records, Ramban's declaration and Ibn Ezra's birth dates; Levine "registered their affiliations"); declared their lineage (Ramban's reading).
4. 1:53, mishmeret. The shipped units use "charge". Options: **keep "charge"** (recommended: Levine's duty sense, same tracked thread `charge`; the Greek and Ramban's guarding go in a note); guard duty (Milgrom, Ramban, Ibn Ezra).
